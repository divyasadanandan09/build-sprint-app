IDEA LOCK · Build Sprint

The idea, in one line: A WhatsApp referral agent that checks in with a fitness trainer's clients after their fourth week and turns their replies, by voice note or text, into neighbour-style recommendations they forward to their own society groups in one tap.
Why me (at least 1 of 3: an audience that trusts me, years inside the workflow, data nobody else has): 12+ years in growth and product marketing, with CRM and lifecycle marketing at Fi Money, Practo and redBus, so trigger-based nudges and referral loops are work I've run at scale. I now run my own product marketing consultancy. I also live in the same society as my first user, so I can watch the loop run in the real group it's meant for.

GOAL
The one goal they hire it for (money, time, status or life): Money. More enquiries from nearby homes, without paying for ads.
Delta 4 (the steps today → the steps with my product):
- Today: the trainer notices a happy client → remembers to ask → asks awkwardly in person → the client says "sure" → the trainer follows up → the client doesn't know what to write → forgets → nothing gets shared.
- With my product: the client hits week 4 → the AI asks "How's it going?" on WhatsApp → the client replies however she likes, as a voice note or a text, in any language → if the reply is too thin (e.g. "Good! Loving it 😊"), the AI asks one short follow-up, like "What's changed for you since you started?" → the AI turns her own words into a short neighbour-style message → she forwards it to her society group in one tap.
- Trainer steps after onboarding: zero. Client steps: one or two replies, one tap.
The sin it rides (optional): Sloth: the client replies the way she already chats, by voice or a quick text, and never has to sit down and write a recommendation. Pride: she gets to be the neighbour who found the good studio.

USER
The trigger (when the pain hits):
- Trainer: enquiries stay flat even after flyers and posts in the society WhatsApp group.
- Client: week 4, when results first show and enthusiasm peaks.
Today's path, step by step (including not solving it at all): The trainer posts her own ads in society WhatsApp groups and hands out pamphlets, which residents skim past as ads. Sometimes she asks a happy client to recommend her. The client agrees and never does it. Most weeks nobody asks at all, and word of mouth happens only by chance.
Who they trust on this decision:
- Trainer (buyer): herself.
- Prospects: neighbours in their own society group, far more than anything the trainer posts about herself.
Would they pay? (what exists today that people pay for): Yes. Senja and Testimonial.to charge $25 to $50 a month just to collect testimonials. Local trainers already pay for pamphlets and Instagram boosts that don't convert. One extra client a month covers the cost.

PRODUCT
Onboarding (how a first-time user feels the value fastest): The trainer shares her client list with join dates. Every client already past week 4 gets the check-in the same day, so the first forwarded recommendation lands in a society group within 24 hours.
The core loop (user stories, written by me):
- Context: Mayuri is a Zumba and strength trainer who has run her studio for over a year. Fewer than 10% of the ~400 homes within 1 km come to her studio, and enquiries are low, but 90%+ of demos convert to paying clients. Her gap is enquiries, not conversion.
- As a client in week 4, I get a WhatsApp message asking how it's going. I reply the way I normally would, with a quick voice note or a few lines of text in Kannada, Hindi or English. If I just say "Good!", it asks me one easy follow-up about what's changed for me. I get back a short message in my own words and forward it to my society group in one tap.
- As Mayuri, I see who forwarded and which enquiries came in, without having asked anyone myself.
- As a neighbour, I see a message from someone I know, not an ad, and message Mayuri for a demo.
Coming back (optional): More triggers on the same loop: week 12, a visible milestone (weight, stamina, first full class), or a client's anniversary. New joiners keep the loop running on their own. Mayuri gets a monthly recap of forwards and enquiries.
The AI-first part (onboarding, engagement or the core loop): The core loop. Take the AI out and nobody asks, nobody follows up, nobody writes, nothing gets forwarded. The AI does the asking twice: it opens the conversation at week 4, and it asks a follow-up when a reply is too thin to write from. Edges: it shows up at the trigger moment and works from the client's own words, in whatever form and language she replies in.

MARKET
Tailwinds (where funding is going, what Google Trends shows, timing): Many new retail outlets and studios are opening in Sarjapur, Bangalore, so they all compete for the same society groups. Speech-to-text now handles regional-language voice notes well, so clients can reply by voice or text in their own language and the output still reads naturally. Society WhatsApp groups are already where neighbours ask "anyone know a good...?"
